{%- comment -%} Interactive map of U.S. fusion research sites, as built for usfusionenergy.org (Mapbox GL, started by assets/js/fusion-map.js). The locations are a copy of that site's data in /assets/data/fusion-locations.geojson. Needs mapbox_token in _config.yml. Without one, the fallback image renders like a media-full image. With one, the fallback covers the map box until the map has loaded, and stays if it can't (no WebGL). Params: fallback (image under /assets/img/), alt (for the fallback), caption, border=false to leave off the hairline border. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign fallback_src = '/assets/img/' | append: include.fallback | relative_url -%} {%- assign fallback_key = '/assets/img/' | append: include.fallback | replace: '//', '/' -%} {%- if include.border == false or include.border == "false" -%} {%- assign borderless = " is-borderless" -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign borderless = "" -%} {%- endif -%}
