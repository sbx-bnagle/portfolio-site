{%- comment -%} A quoted post or article as real text rather than a screenshot, so it scales with the reader's font size, can be selected and searched, and reads properly in a screen reader. Params: author the account or publication doing the talking author_url links the name handle @name for a post, or the writer's byline for an article handle_url links the handle; falls back to author_url avatar image under /assets/img/, shown left of the name url links the name when no author_url is given text the quote itself (markup allowed) src + alt an image the post carried The card shows the quote and its source; dates are not displayed. Wrap two or three in
...
to sit them side by side. Usage: {% include tweet.html author="Zap Energy" handle="@Energy_Zap" author_url="https://x.com/Energy_Zap" avatar="few/icon/zap.jpg" text="..." %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign name_link = include.author_url | default: include.url -%} {%- assign handle_link = include.handle_url | default: name_link -%} {%- comment -%} Link the @mentions inside the quote. Trailing punctuation stays outside the link, so "@PPPLab." links the account and keeps the full stop as text. mention_base overrides where they point. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign mention_base = include.mention_base | default: "https://x.com/" -%} {%- assign quote = "" -%} {%- assign words = include.text | split: " " -%} {%- for word in words -%} {%- assign lead = word | slice: 0, 1 -%} {%- if lead == "@" and word.size > 1 -%} {%- assign handle = word -%} {%- assign trailing = "" -%} {%- assign tail = word | slice: -1 -%} {%- if ".,!?:;" contains tail -%} {%- assign cut = word.size | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign handle = word | slice: 0, cut -%} {%- assign trailing = tail -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign account = handle | remove_first: "@" -%} {%- capture piece -%}{{ handle }}{{ trailing }}{%- endcapture -%} {%- else -%} {%- capture piece -%}{{ word }}{%- endcapture -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign quote = quote | append: piece -%} {%- unless forloop.last -%}{%- assign quote = quote | append: " " -%}{%- endunless -%} {%- endfor -%}
