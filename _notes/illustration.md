RIP Symmetry Magazine, an awesome resource for accessible introductions to contemporary particle physics. It&rsquo;s still online at [symmetrymagazine.org](https://www.symmetrymagazine.org/) for a little longer, but will be retired any day now. Sandbox Studio, where I worked, art directed the magazine for over 20 years. I illustrated a handful of articles over my time there. Here are a handful of my favorites.
