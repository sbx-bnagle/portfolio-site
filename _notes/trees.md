Requesting trees to be planted through Chicago&rsquo;s 311 service ***INCLUDE LINK*** is a hobby of mine. In the past I&rsquo;ve used Google Street View to find parkway spaces in need of tree and fire off a request. Part brag part confession, I&rsquo;ve made thousands of these requests. It's a slow process, but mindless and easy to do at the end of the day.

Recently I put together a tool to speed this way up. I had Claude develop a program that performs an initial assessment of potential sites and eliminate obvious duds. It wasn&rsquo;t able to reliably identify planting sposts, so the remainder are presented to me Hot-or-Not ***ref*** style for quick review. I&rsquo;m reviewing potential spaces by ward, but intend to the whole city (currently on the 22nd Ward out of 50). Once compiled the requests will be submitted programatically to 311.

Since the requests are compiled before submitting this allows for a comprehensive plan for species of trees to ber planted. Currently, you can ask for a tree from a list provided by Streets and San per request. Unfortunately, requests made piecemeal like this result in a lack of diversity, shade, an habitats for pollinators. By compiling ahead of time, I&rsquo;ll be able suggest a plan that meets diversity targets, restores dwindling Oak numbers, provides fast-growing shade in the hottest neighborhoods, and efficiently filters particulates for residential areas near the freeway.

Of course, just because I make the requests doesn&rsquo;t mean a tree gets planted. The owners of adjacent lot get to weigh in and it can take time for Streets and San to get to the requests. But! the results to my requests so far have been encouraging.
