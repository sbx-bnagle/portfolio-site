Like most designers, I&rsquo;m a big fan of Dutch design, including perennial favorites like [Karel Martens](https://martens-martens.com/), [Wim Crouwel](https://www.neugraphic.com/wim/index.html), and [Experimental Jetset](https://www.jetset.nl/). But for me, [Joost Grootens](https://www.joostgrootens.nl/) is the <span role="img" aria-label="GOAT, greatest of all time">🐐</span>.

What draws me most is his pragmatism. Grootens is known for information design, especially atlases, and he prioritizes large clear gestures and getting the information across over polish. Hierarchy often comes from spacing rather than subtle changes to the type&rsquo;s size or weight, legend symbols are included in the running text, a chart&rsquo;s line climbs into the paragraph above it, and key features spill past the edge of the map when they need to. I relate to the challenge of making technical material approachable, and his atlases are my personal favorite answer to it.
